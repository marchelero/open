## Phasing Logic
Three independently mergeable phases, each ending at a "green" gate. Critical bugs (P1) ship first because they are user-visible and small. Drift/redundancy (P2) is foundation work — accurate counts and a single router must exist before M1 re-points commands. Scaffolding (P3) is additive and does not touch existing dispatch.
